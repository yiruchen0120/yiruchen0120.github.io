**Authors:** Yiru Chen, Muhammad Ali Nasir, and Xingjie Wei

**Status:** Working Paper

### Abstract

This paper investigates how emotional signals conveyed through the textual, vocal, and facial dimensions of Federal Reserve Chair communication influence international financial markets.

Using multimodal emotion measures and high-frequency financial market data, we examine the international spillovers of central bank communication and their heterogeneity across countries.

The analysis further explores cross-modal interactions, global risk conditions, and the role of country-specific economic vulnerabilities.
